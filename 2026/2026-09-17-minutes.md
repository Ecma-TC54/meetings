## 5 Key Discussions

### 5.1 VERS draft

Michael Herzog presented a recent draft of the VERS standard in PDF format, produced with ecmarkup. There’s currently [a pair of](https://github.com/package-url/vers-spec/issues/140) [open issues](https://github.com/package-url/vers-spec/issues/143) to clarify that VERS 1<sup>st</sup> edition applies strictly to linear versions, not version trees or graphs, the relevant text will be added to the scope clause and the beginning of clause 5.

The committee has committed to reviewing the VERS draft ahead of an ad-hoc meeting on 06 October 2026 where, upon reaching consensus, the document will be approved for submission to the GA.

In the interim, new issues can be filed [on GitHub](https://github.com/Ecma-TC54/ECMA-xxx-VERS).

### 5.2 Ad-hoc meetings for standard approval

The committee will reconvene on 24 September and again on 06 October to ensure that all standards intended for submission to the GA in December are ready for the royalty-free opt-out period. In the meantime, the chair will create specific feedback threads in slack for each of the three intended publications (VERS, PURL, and TEA) to ensure a rapid feedback loop.

The secretary will provide editorial reviews as requested.
