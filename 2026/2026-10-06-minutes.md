# 4 Key discussion

## 4.1 PURL review

**Michael Herzog** (AboutCode Europe ASBL) began by sharing a link to the latest draft PDF of the second edition of ECMA-427 PURL. He produced [release notes](https://github.com/package-url/purl-spec/blob/main/docs/specification/ECMA-427-2nd_Edition-Release_notes.md) of major changes and normative edits for the purpose of discussion within the committee.

Primarily the changes provide clearer language overall. Additionally, two new informative Annexes provide alternative representations of the specification. It is the hope of the committee that those Annexes are able to shift to normative once they have proven themselves useful and accurate. As Michael reviewed the changes as described in the release notes, **Olle Johansson** (OWASP) raised a question regarding a gap between slashes being permitted when sending vs receiving. Jan Kowalleck (OWASP) explained that the change was for the purpose of documenting existing behaviour, and should not necessarily lead to any additional requirements.

## 4.2 PURL approval

Prior to a formal call for approval, Olle took a moment to recognize the ABNS Annex, which got support from all attendees.

Steve called for approval and received explicit assent from the attendees. ECMA-427 PURL 2<sup>nd</sup> edition was approved.

## 4.3 VERS review

Michael Herzog presented the current draft of the VERS standard. He pointed out that the VEDS standard does not cover tree-based version ranges, only linear. There may be a future version where tree-based version ranges are attempted. The only remaining open issue is what specific link to be used for normatively referencing JSON schema, which the secretary volunteered to manage.

## 4.4 VERS approval

Steve called for approval and received explicit assent from the attendees. VERS was approved to submit to the Ecma GA in December for publication.
