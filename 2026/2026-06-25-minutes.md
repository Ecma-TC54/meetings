## Key discussion

### CycloneDX Specification Updates

Committee was reminded that several significant proposals are expected for review during upcoming meetings. Pre-reading materials will be distributed in advance to facilitate review.

The announcement highlighted the upcoming July pre‑read of the *X2 Modeling Branch Review Guide for ECMA TC54*, updates that introduces a wide range of new features. These include expanded modeling for risks, threats and weaknesses; one breaking change related to vulnerabilities; new blueprint structures describing how systems are assembled; and behaviour models capturing both intent and observable actions.

This represents a major evolution of the specification, members were asked to review the document in advance. An issue (PR) has been created summarizing the key elements and linking to the relevant schemas to provide context during review. The feature has been under development for approximately eighteen months, with contributions from leading experts in both security and privacy modeling.

Members are expected to come prepared to vote on the feature at the July meeting.

### Migration from JSF to JSS (X.590)

CycloneDX 2.0 will migrate from JSON Signature Format (JSF) to JSS (X.590). JSF is not a formal standard; JSS is a formal standard. The implementation in CycloneDX 2.0 is verbatim from the standard. This is a breaking change:

* JSF will no longer be supported in 2.0.
* **JSS** will be required.

Libraries continue to exist to support both formats for backward compatibility.

Consensus reached; change approved.

### Normative Change: Conditional Components

Introduced to support hardware industry realities where component inventories vary due to supply constraints. Discussion included implications for security analysis and CRA compliance.

* CycloneDX will support boolean operators (AND, OR, XOR) within component inventories.
* Allows BOMs to express conditional or alternative components.

Consensus reached; change approved.

### Normative Change: Party Model

CycloneDX will consolidate multiple role specific fields (supplier, manufacturer, author, etc.) into a unified Party Model.

* A Party may be: Organization, Person, System, Persona

New features include:

* Legal name, Founding/dissolution dates, Jurisdiction, Aliases and former names, Role definitions (predefined + custom), Ordering of suppliers, Coordinates for physical locations
* Two “other” fields identified for removal; approval contingent on their deletion.

Consensus reached; change approved with conditions.

### Normative Change: Component Identity

Replaces individual identifier fields (CPE, SWID, etc.) with a unified identities array. Supports multiple schemes including GS1, EPC, manufacturer part numbers, model numbers, and custom schemes. Includes party assertions for identifiers.

Consensus reached; change approved.
